This feed aggregates content from AI researchers, engineers, and commentators representing diverse perspectives on artificial intelligence—from deep skeptics to cautious optimists. The goal is to present a balanced view of the ongoing discourse around AI capabilities, safety, and societal impact.

Gary Marcus

Cognitive Scientist, AI Researcher, Author
AI Skeptic

Professor emeritus at NYU and founder of Geometric Intelligence (acquired by Uber). Marcus is one of the most prominent critics of current deep learning approaches, arguing that LLMs lack true understanding and that the path to AGI requires hybrid neurosymbolic approaches combining neural networks with classical AI. He frequently challenges hype around AI capabilities and timelines.

Simon Willison

Independent AI Researcher, Creator of Datasette
Pragmatic Practitioner

Co-creator of the Django web framework and prolific open-source developer. Willison approaches AI from a practical, hands-on perspective—extensively documenting his experiments with LLMs and building tools around them. He's neither a hype-man nor a doomer, instead focusing on what these tools can actually do today, their limitations, and responsible usage patterns. His blog is an invaluable resource for understanding LLM capabilities through rigorous testing.

Andrej Karpathy

AI Researcher, Former Director of AI at Tesla, OpenAI Founding Member
Technical Optimist

One of the most influential figures in deep learning, Karpathy led Tesla's Autopilot vision team and was a founding member of OpenAI. Known for his exceptional ability to explain complex AI concepts accessibly, his "Neural Networks: Zero to Hero" series has educated thousands. He's bullish on AI capabilities while maintaining technical rigor, often sharing insights on where the field is heading based on first-principles understanding.

François Chollet

AI Researcher at Google, Creator of Keras
Technical Critic

Creator of Keras (the popular deep learning framework) and researcher at Google. Chollet is known for his ARC benchmark, designed to test genuine reasoning vs. pattern matching in AI systems. He's skeptical of claims that scaling alone will lead to AGI, arguing that current LLMs demonstrate memorization and interpolation rather than true intelligence. His perspective combines deep technical expertise with philosophical rigor about what "intelligence" means.

Steve Yegge

Software Engineer, Tech Industry Veteran
Enthusiastic Adopter

Legendary software engineer known for his long-form technical essays and stints at Amazon, Google, and Grab. Yegge has become one of the most vocal proponents of "vibe coding"—using AI coding agents to dramatically accelerate software development. His projects like Beads and Gas Town push the boundaries of agentic AI workflows. He believes AI is transforming software development at an exponential pace and that developers who don't adapt will be left behind.

LessWrong

Community Blog (Filtered: Eliezer Yudkowsky, Paul Christiano)
AI Safety Focus

LessWrong is a community blog focused on rationality and AI safety. Eliezer Yudkowsky, founder of MIRI, is perhaps the most prominent voice warning about existential risks from advanced AI, arguing that alignment is extremely difficult and that we may be racing toward catastrophe. Paul Christiano, former OpenAI researcher and founder of the Alignment Research Center, takes a more measured but still concerned approach to AI safety, focusing on practical alignment research.
